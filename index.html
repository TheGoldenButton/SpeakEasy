🎡 Emoji wheel

Press the index trigger on either controller (desktop: press L) to open the wheel — it opens in that hand and stays open; press again to close it and send. The golden ball rests in the centre. Point your other controller at it, hold its trigger (desktop: mouse button) and drag toward an emoji on the rim until the ball turns green, then let go to pick. Let go anywhere else and it returns to the centre. Closing the wheel sends your picks above your head for a few seconds; you can see it in the mirror.

🧚 Ask Ember

With the wheel closed, hold the index trigger on its own (desktop: hold L), speak your question, then let go. A quick tap still opens the wheel. A ball of light forms while you speak, then Ember hears you and acts on it — she can build, change the world, answer, or just play (see the Ember agent section). With no API key she only understands a few simple build requests.

🧚 Ember the agent fairy

Ember is a living agent. Ask or tell her anything (hold the trigger / L and speak, or type below) and she will decide what to do: build structures, animate and recolor things, change the sky and lighting, play sounds, move the person, remember things, or write her own code. She can take pictures of the scene from any angle (front, top, your own view…) to check how things look, and place or smoothly move objects precisely. She shows her thinking as the glowing orb and answers above her head with emoji and/or a speech bubble, so she can chat and answer questions as well as build; nothing is projected in the sky. In Parent mode she only answers with building and emoji — no text bubbles. She can also look things up on the internet (Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo, or your own search endpoint via MANUAL_CONFIG.SEARCH_URL) and read web pages to answer current or factual questions (off in Parent mode). She only acts when you ask — or when you tell her to repeat something (e.g. “every 5 minutes, add a new star to the sky”). Everything she builds is saved in this browser and shared live with anyone in your room. Any code she writes runs in a locked sandbox with no access to your keys or this page.

🎙️ Translate to emoji

Hands only: with the wheel closed, pinch both index fingers together and hold. Controllers: hold index + grip together on one controller with the wheel closed (desktop: hold V), or hold the trigger of the wheel hand while the wheel is open (desktop: hold L with the wheel open). Speak, then let go to send. Wave your free hand through the transcript box (desktop: Esc) to cancel.

📷 AR / VR

Tap AR Mode at the top (or the 📷 button in-headset) to switch between the virtual room and the real world through the headset cameras, without leaving the headset. Ask Ember uses that real-world view. Room fit: while in AR the headset scans your walls, ceiling height, tables, couches, beds, doors and windows. When you leave AR the virtual room resizes to your real space with stand-ins for that furniture.

🕹️ Moving & pages

The left thumbstick (desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk, Shift to hurry, drag the mouse to look) walks you around, even with the wheel open. In AR mode the thumbstick (and hand-gesture walking, snap-turn and jump) is switched off — you simply walk around your real home, and Ember stays inside your real walls and walks around your furniture. The right thumbstick left/right (desktop: Q/E, arrows or the mouse wheel while the wheel is open) flips wheel pages, or snap-turns you when the wheel is closed. A (right controller) / Space (desktop) jumps. B / Y (desktop: Esc) closes the wheel without sending; desktop Enter sends, Backspace or right-click removes the last emoji. Hands only: with the wheel open, pinch the middle fingers of both hands to close it without sending, and middle-pinch your free hand and swipe left/right to flip pages. Move a hand forward through the air to walk forward and backward to walk back; twist your wrist like a knob (clockwise = turn right, anticlockwise = turn left) to turn; flick both palms up to jump; pull both hands in hard to dash; with flight on, flap both palms up to rise and press both down to sink. Pinches are for the wheel and Ember.

🖐️ Hands-only guide

Click / tap: pinch thumb + index finger.
Emoji wheel: quick index pinch (wheel closed) opens it; pinch again to send. Pick: pinch the golden ball with your other hand, drag to an emoji, release. Flip pages: middle-finger pinch your free hand and swipe. Cancel: middle-finger pinch with both hands.
Ask Ember: pinch and hold half a second, let go, then talk — it sends when you stop (pinch again to send now). Translate: pinch both index fingers, let go, talk. Cancel a listening box: wave your free hand through it.

Walk: hand forward = forward; hand backward = back. Turn: twist your wrist like a knob (clockwise = right). Jump: flick both palms up. Dash: pull both hands in hard. Fly (if enabled): flap both palms up to rise, press both palms down to sink.

🙂 Your avatar

You are a stick man sized to your real height in the headset: start sitting and he starts small at that sitting height; stand up and he grows to your standing height at once. He only shrinks again if you squat / stay sat down well below your height for more than 3 seconds — a quick duck or bend keeps your full height. Its mouth is a line that turns into an O while you speak (microphone lip-sync; headsets with WebXR face tracking drive it directly). Arms follow your controllers or hands. Its face shows the emotion of the emoji you send — smiles, frowns, angry brows, tears, blushing, heart eyes, sunglasses and more — stepping through your message in time with the symbols, then relaxing. Sound effects can be switched off and on in settings.

🎮 Games, portals & grabbing

Ask Ember for a game (tic-tac-toe, ring toss, pong…), a portal to a new world, or things to play with. Grab: VR – squeeze the grip near an object, or point at it and pull the trigger to pull it into your hand; release to throw/drop. Desktop/touch – drag it. Tap buttons and portals by pointing and pressing the trigger / clicking. Walk through a portal to travel; the 🏠 button (or the exit portal) brings you home. Ember takes its turns in games automatically after your moves (needs the API key or proxy).

🌐 Multiplayer

Tap 👥 (top left). Private room: “Host private room” gives you an invite link/code — only people with it can join, and everything Ember builds there (and the sky) is shared live with everyone in it. Online mode (⚙️ settings, the 👥 panel, or 🌐 in the headset settings) drops you into one endless open world shared with everyone who has it on: you appear somewhere near another online person, but you have to explore and build to find each other. Your open-world builds are yours — others see them but can't change them; the sky stays personal. Rooms have no player limit: everyone links peer-to-peer to their nearest neighbours and updates hop across the mesh. 🎤 is spatial voice chat. Parent mode (⚙️) turns off voice and online mode.

Drift around the white platform as it flies through space, and check yourself out in the mirror. WASD / left stick moves, drag looks around.

Settings

API — key, endpoints and model

One Meta API key is used for both Muse Voice Transcribe and Muse Ember. It is stored only in this browser (localStorage), never in this file. With a key: speech goes to Muse Voice Transcribe, the text goes to Muse Ember. Without a key, voice falls back to the headset's built-in speech recognition (the same engine as the keyboard's mic button, started automatically with no keyboard popping up) and Ember falls back to a small local phrase translator.

Ember receives one message: for translation, a short instruction to translate what you said into an emoji sequence of 12 or less; for Ask Ember (trigger-hold, wheel closed), an instruction to actually answer your question helpfully as a separate assistant, using the scene image for context. Lewd or hateful content is not translated.

Reset room wipes everything: all objects, portals and worlds Ember made, game state, Ember\u2019s memory, repeating tasks and conversation, and restores the original sky, lighting, gravity and your position (tap twice to confirm).

Online mode stays on this page. Extra TURN relay and Parent mode are collapsed just above these buttons once multiplayer starts. In the headset, ⚙️ opens the same controls on General, API, Network and Parent tabs.

Before you enter

SpeakEasy can use cloud AI for voice and Ask Ember. If you allow it: your microphone audio is sent for transcription, the text goes to Muse Ember, and for Ask Ember a picture of what you are looking at (in AR, a headset camera frame) is attached. These go to this app's proxy server (or Meta directly if you add your own key in settings). Nothing is sent until you hold the trigger to speak.

If you stay offline, voice uses the headset's built-in speech recognition and Ember uses a small local phrase translator, and no audio or images leave this page. You can change this later in settings.
